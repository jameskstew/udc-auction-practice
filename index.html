Understanding Digital Capitalism: practise the auction

Practise the auction

Try the market on your own: no sign-in, no table, nothing saved. You bid on your own against seven simulated rivals with different strategies (in class, the auction is run for groups), and every sale is explained step by step.

Game A: one firm per sale
The format we use in class. Sealed bids, a falling clock or lottery tickets; rent for players still without a firm; change any rule and see what happens.

Ask an AI to explain it

Paste the full rules into a chatbot and ask it to explain a sale, or suggest a strategy, after you play.

Things to try: what strategy wins most often? What happens to the operator's income when you change the price rule or the rent? Who gains from a falling clock?
